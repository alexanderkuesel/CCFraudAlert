Fixed expenses

Monthly costs that never reach your card: rent, school fees, transfers, cash. Each one is booked on its day every month from From through Until (leave empty to keep going), and shows up as a tag in its category, counting toward that category's budget. To record a price change, set Until on the old row and add a new one, so earlier months keep the old amount.
